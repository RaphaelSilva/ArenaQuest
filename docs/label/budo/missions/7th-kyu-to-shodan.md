Rules shared by every mission are in [README.md](README.md#design-rules); in short:

- `sequential`, `assigned` to the grade's group, 2026-11-01 00:00 BRT → 2027-10-31 23:59 BRT;
- "watch N videos" is `video_watched` with `minCount = min(ready videos, 50)`;
- a step titled `Module — Sub-folder` targets the sub-folder, whose videos do not count for the module;
- step XP = 10 per required video (minimum 50); mission XP = 500.
